History, 1993–2017

Twenty years of shows, year by year.

From Waleed Al-Qadoumi's first years in Kuwaiti exhibitions to a marketplace in Toronto. Each entry names its source: the TopExpo company profile or the press at the time.

1993
19932017
  1. 1993

    Where the experience starts

    Waleed Al-Qadoumi begins working in Kuwait's exhibition, conference, media and public relations industry.

    TopExpo company profile
  2. 1998

    TopExpo is founded in Kuwait

    Founded by Waleed Al-Qadoumi and Sheikh Malek Al Sabah. The Real Estate & Investment Exhibition opens at the Regency Hotel and runs twice a year until 2007.

    TopExpo company profile
  3. 2000

    Homex

    An annual interiors exhibition at the Regency Hotel, held until 2006, with 30 to 40 exhibitors each year.

    TopExpo company profile
  4. 2001

    Gulf Real Estate, Money & Investment Exhibition

    GCC property and financial companies at Al-Rayyah Hall, in 2001 and again in 2005.

    TopExpo company profile
  5. 2003

    Jobs Exhibitions

    Organised in 2003 and 2004 for Kuwait's Manpower and Government Restructuring Programme. TopExpo handles promotion, set-up and delivery.

    TopExpo company profile
  6. 2004

    Elite Real Estate Exhibition

    A focused annual property exhibition, held from 2004 to 2006.

    TopExpo company profile
  7. 200818–19 March

    First International Conference for Islamic Sukuk

    Held at the Radisson SAS in the Kingdom of Bahrain.

    Original conference programme, reproduced in the TopExpo company profile
  8. 2010

    Sales and business development

    Mohammed Qadoumi becomes Executive Manager for Sales and Business Development across Top Real Estate Group and Top Expo, a role he holds until 2017.

    TREG Holdings website, tregholdings.com
  9. 20129–14 April

    RIE in Mishref

    Top Expo opens the Real Estate and Investment Exhibition at the fairground in Mishref.

    Al Qabas, 9 April 2012 (TREG Holdings press archive, p. 66)
  10. 2015

    KIPS 2015

    More than 250 projects from over 15 countries. Waleed Al-Qadoumi speaks for the group as Managing Director.

    Zawya, KIPS 2015 coverage
  11. 20167–12 November

    The ninth KIPS

    Sixty companies show 250 projects from 15 countries. The Toronto marketplace is announced at the show.

    Al Qabas, 13 October 2016 (TREG Holdings press archive, p. 21); Al Qabas, 3 November 2016 (TREG Holdings press archive, p. 18)
  12. 20168 November

    Agreement with Informa Exhibitions

    RIE and KIPS are to run in 2017 as Cityscape Kuwait events at the Kuwait International Fairgrounds.

    Kuwait Times, 8 November 2016, "Cityscape joins Top Expo Group"
  13. 201716–18 May

    Toronto

    The Canadian International Real Estate Marketplace opens at the Metro Toronto Convention Centre. Sixty real estate companies take part.

    TopExpo company profile
