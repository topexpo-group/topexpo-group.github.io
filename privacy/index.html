Privacy · Updated 4 November 2026

How we use your details.

A short notice about the enquiry form, the only place on this site where you give us personal details.

What we collect

The enquiry form asks for your name, organisation, phone number, email, city and a short description of your event. You decide what to fill in. Only your name and one way to reach you are required.

What we do not collect

This site sets no cookies. It uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not record which pages you visit.

Why we use your details

Only to answer your enquiry and to discuss the event with you. We do not sell your details or share them for marketing.

How your enquiry reaches us

The form prepares the text of your enquiry on your device. Nothing is sent from this page until delivery is connected.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to reply and follow up on the event, then delete it.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details you sent us, use the enquiry form. We answer every day.

Planning an exhibition or a conference?

Tell us the sector, the city and roughly when. We answer every day, around the clock.
