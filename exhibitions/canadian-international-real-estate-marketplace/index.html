Property & investment · Toronto · May 2017

Canadian International Real Estate Marketplace

In May 2017 TopExpo took its format to Toronto, with developers it knew from the Kuwait shows.

Venue
Metro Toronto Convention Centre
Dates
16 to 18 May 2017
Exhibitors
60 real estate companies
Announced
At KIPS, November 2016
Coverage
Kuwait Times, Arab Times and Arabic-language press

The Canadian International Real Estate Marketplace opened at the Metro Toronto Convention Centre on 16 May 2017 and ran until 18 May. Sixty real estate companies took part.

Tridel and Lennar, developers that had exhibited at TopExpo's shows in Kuwait, took part again in Toronto.

The marketplace was announced in Kuwait in November 2016, during KIPS, together with a promotion agreement.

Mohammed Qadoumi, then Executive Manager for Sales and Business Development, led the marketplace for the group.
