Real Estate & Investment Exhibition

The show TopExpo opened with in 1998. It brought developers together with the builders, suppliers and banks that finance a purchase.

Organiser
Top Expo Group
First venue
Regency Hotel, Kuwait
Format
Twice a year, 1998 to 2007
Exhibitors
Developers, construction firms, building-material suppliers, banks and finance companies
Properties shown
From 15 to 20 countries
2012 venue
Fairground, Mishref

The Real Estate & Investment Exhibition opened at the Regency Hotel in 1998 and ran there twice a year until 2007.

Developers met construction firms, building-material suppliers, banks and financing services. Properties from 15 to 20 countries were shown, according to the company profile.

In 2012 the show ran at the fairground in Mishref, from 9 to 14 April.

Under the agreement with Informa Exhibitions, the 2017 edition was scheduled as Cityscape Kuwait RIE, 10 to 15 April, at the Kuwait International Fairgrounds.
