Toronto · May 2017

The Kuwait shows travel to North America.

In May 2017 TopExpo held the Canadian International Real Estate Marketplace in Toronto. Tridel and Lennar, developers that had exhibited at TopExpo's shows in Kuwait, took part again.

Services

Organisation, stand design, advertising, media relations and conference management under one roof, so exhibitors deal with a single team.

How we work
  1. 01

    Exhibition organisation

    Property, investment, interiors and careers exhibitions, from the event concept and floor plan to opening day.

  2. 02

    Stands and pavilions

    Stands and pavilions for exhibitors, designed and built for the venue, in Kuwait and across the Gulf.

  3. 03

    Advertising and media relations
